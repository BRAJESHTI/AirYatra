The user is building AirYatra, a PAN-India aviation booking platform. The project's scope has significantly expanded to include a comprehensive **Landing Infrastructure Intelligence System**.

This system must:
1.  **Classify Landing Points:** Differentiate between Airports, Government Helipads, Private Helipads, and Village/Private Land.

7.  **Enable and Verify Profiles:** Enable Google Login and ensure profiles for Customers, Operators, and Helipad Owners are created and functional.
