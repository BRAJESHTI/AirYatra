The user wants to build AirYatra, a comprehensive air mobility aggregator platform. The application is designed to connect customers with helicopter and charter operators across India.

The project is divided into several user-facing panels, each with a rich feature set:
- **Super Admin / Admin Panel:** Full control over the platform, including dashboards, user/role management, operator verification, booking management, multi-level approvals, landing permission (NOC) management, finance/settlements, and audit logs.
- **Regional Manager Panel:** A restricted view for regional oversight, including a regional dashboard and approval authority.
- **Operator Portal:** Allows operators to manage their profile, documents, fleet, pilots, receive and quote inquiries, execute bookings, and view settlements.
- **Customer Portal:** Enables customers to log in, search/book flights, upload landing permissions, make payments, manage trips, and handle cancellations/refunds.
